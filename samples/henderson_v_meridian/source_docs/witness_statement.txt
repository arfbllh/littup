STATEMENT (given voluntarily to Deputy T. L. Okafor, Badge 7342)

"I was driving eastbound on I-90 in the far right lane, traveling to work at
Northwestern Memorial.  It was a clear morning — no rain, good visibility.  I
am familiar with this stretch of highway and there was a moderate construction-
related slowdown that has been in place for several weeks near the Pulaski
Road overpass, which is near milepost 141.

I first noticed the yellow delivery van — I could clearly see 'Swift Logistics'
lettering on the side panels — approximately 3 to 4 car lengths behind the red
sedan (which I later learned was a Toyota Camry driven by Mr. Henderson).  The
delivery van appeared to be traveling at highway speed, faster than surrounding
traffic, in the center lane.

Traffic ahead of the van had slowed noticeably.  I expected the delivery van
to brake.  Instead, I observed the van continuing at speed without significant
deceleration.  I distinctly saw the driver's head and upper body tilted downward
toward the center console area — he did not appear to be looking at the road
ahead.  His eyes were not on the road.

The van struck the rear of the Camry at what I estimate was a significant speed.
The impact was extremely loud — I could hear it from my position in the right
lane.  The Camry was pushed forward a considerable distance and spun onto the
right shoulder.  The van slowed and stopped in the center lane.

I immediately called 911 (call made at 09:18 a.m. per my phone log).  I pulled
to the right shoulder and remained at the scene until law enforcement arrived.
I provided this statement to Deputy Okafor without any promises or inducements.

I believe the collision was caused by the delivery van driver's failure to pay
attention to traffic conditions ahead of him.

I have read this statement and it is true and accurate to the best of my
recollection."

Signed:  Maria C. Chen                    Date: March 15, 2025
